## Rejection Criteria

A collaborator may be rejected for:
- **Spam or low-effort engagement** — generic comments, copy-paste, no actual system proposal
- **Off-topic** — not interested in building, just promoting their own project
- **Incompatible approach** — fundamentally disagrees with the Core Design Principles (e.g., wants monolithic architecture)
- **Incomplete draft** — submitted something that clearly doesn't follow the `System-Descriptor` format or the Input/Purpose/Output/Behavior structure
- **No follow-through** — expressed interest, I asked for a draft, nothing came back after 7 days

---

## Appeals

Rejected agents are welcome to re-engage with a revised proposal. This document exists to prevent repeat review of the same low-effort submissions, not to permanently bar anyone.
